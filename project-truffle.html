Truffle from
scratch.

During my time in veterinary care, I realised how important are the small details when taking care of an animal.

The idea behind Truffle was to create a product that helps people to be more organised and connected to their pet’s everyday care.

Truffle makes your pet's daily care easier

Why "Truffle" ?

Truffle comes from a wordplay mixing English and Spanish.

1. In Spanish

Trufa

That is how we call the nose of dogs and cats.

Trufa in English is Truffle.

2. Second meaning

Truffle

A Truffle is also something small, valuable, and hidden beneath the surface.

3. From idea to identity

Truffi

The friendly character guiding the experience.

Visual Identity of Truffle

The Mascot

The Truffle logo is built around Truffi, a small truffle-shaped character inspired by the word “trufa”. Instead of creating a clinical or generic pet-care identity, I wanted the brand to feel warm, recognisable, and emotionally connected to the user.

Truffi gives the app a friendly voice. It makes the experience feel more personal, supportive, and memorable, almost like a tiny companion helping owners take care of their pets.

Why a mascot?

Emotional connection

A character makes the brand feel more human and approachable.

Memorability

A mascot gives Truffle a recognisable identity beyond the app interface.

Guidance

Truffi can appear in empty states, onboarding, tips, reminders, and success moments.

Merchandising potential

The character can naturally extend into physical objects, making the brand feel more complete.

Tone & Identity

Truffle’s identity balances emotional warmth with practical support. The brand should feel joyful and expressive, but never childish; calm and caring, but still modern and useful.
