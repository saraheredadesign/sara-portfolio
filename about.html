A quick overview of my timeline

Where everything starts


In 2022, I was working at AniCura Dyresykehus in Oslo, Norway, surrounded by an amazing team and caring for animals every day.

That experience taught me something that still shapes the way I work today: the importance of small details 🤏🏻

In animal care, a subtle change in behaviour, a small sign of discomfort, or one overlooked step in a protocol can be crucial.


Over time, I realised that design works in a similar way. From animal care protocols to app components, the details are often what make the real difference ✨

From UX to real products


I started with UX 🏗️, learning how to understand users, architectures, define problems, map journeys, organize information, create wireframes, and test ideas before moving into visuals.

Then I stepped into UI 🖌️, designing interfaces with a strong UX foundation.

I focused on layout, visual hierarchy, typography, color, components, interaction states, accessibility, and responsive design.

Now, I use both UX and UI to build real digital products 📱⌚️💻, not just ideas. I enjoy turning concepts into clear, functional, and thoughtful experiences people can actually use.

Beyond design

Still learning, still experimenting, and always looking for ways to make things clearer, kinder, and easier to use.
