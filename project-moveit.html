MoveIt

A mobile and smartwatch fitness app designed for quick, equipment-free workouts, helping busy users stay active while tracking real-time health data.

Project Type

Student team project · UX/UI mobile app and smartwatch

Role

UX/UI Designer

Tools

Figma, FlowMapp, Miro, WCAG, Design System principles

Focus Areas

Mobile app, Smartwatch experience, Quick workouts, Heart rate tracking, Calorie tracking, Progress overview

Solution preview

MoveIt connects a mobile fitness app with a smartwatch experience, helping users start short workouts, follow their progress, and track heart rate and calories in real time.

MoveIt was one of my first group projects while studying UX Design. The website shows a curated overview, while the full case study and process can be opened through the Figma link.
